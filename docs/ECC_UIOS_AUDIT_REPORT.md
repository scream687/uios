## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict Refinement

This audit evaluates **UIOS Operating System v3.0** against the **ECC 5 Core Principles**. 

Rather than declaring a single blanket status, this report distinguishes between **Execution Infrastructure** and **Creative Intelligence**:
- **Architecture (Production-Ready)**: The core execution model, immutable `BaseArtifact` lifecycle, SAT constraint solver, `TypedEventBus`, and capability registry are mature, highly tested, and fully frozen.
- **Creative Intelligence (Beta)**: The system demonstrates strong engineering discipline, but is actively building empirical evidence (`CreativeEvidenceSuite`) to demonstrate that its `TasteEngine`, `SlopDetectorEngine`, and `SceneComposerEngine` consistently eliminate AI layout tropes across diverse industries.

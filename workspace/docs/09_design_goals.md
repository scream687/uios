## Design Goals
1. **Uncompromised Contrast**: Pitch Black canvas (`#080808`), Crisp White headlines (`#ffffff`), Electric Lime accent (`#e2ff00`).
2. **Typographic Authority**: ExtraBold display text with `-0.045em` letter-spacing and tight line-heights (`0.98`).
3. **1px Hairline Borders**: Subtle card elevation surfaces (`#131315`) with `border-white/10` or `border-white/15`.
4. **Zero Visual Slop**: Purged of uncurated blur blobs and generic icon boxes.

## Personas Breakdown
1. **Developer**: Condo & commercial builders needing multi-unit site presentations and fast asset deployment.
2. **Luxury Broker**: Independent agents needing high-impact single property links (`estatelink.co/aspen-villa`) to send via WhatsApp or email.
3. **High-Net-Worth Buyer**: Expects 4K video walkthroughs, instant loading, zero email attachment downloads.
4. **Investor**: Focuses on cap rates, projected rental yields, and architectural blueprints.
5. **NRI / International Buyer**: Relies on virtual staging and passcode-gated off-market presentation links.

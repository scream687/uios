## Core Mission
To replace fragmented PDF property brochures, static email attachments, and multi-url MLS links with a single dynamic, trackable visual presentation link for luxury real estate developments.

## Value Proposition
- **Instant Client Engagement**: Zero attachment limits, opens instantly in 4K across mobile & desktop.
- **Architectural Fidelity**: Presents 3D renders, CAD floor plans, and video walkthroughs with uncompromised clarity.
- **Quiet Intelligence**: Real-time buyer engagement analytics without intrusive tracking UI.

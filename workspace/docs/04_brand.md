## Brand Parameters
- **Brand Name**: EstateLink
- **Industry**: Luxury Real Estate Technology
- **Personality**:
  - Luxury
  - Editorial
  - Confident
  - Swiss Minimal
  - Architecture-Inspired
- **Target Audience**: Developers, Builders, Luxury Brokers, High-Net-Worth Buyers

## Explicit Design Rules & Constraints
- **Typography**: Oversized display typography with tight letter-spacing (`-0.045em`).
- **Whitespace**: Heavy, unhurried section padding (`144px` hero rhythm).
- **Palette**: Pitch Black (`#080808` / `#000000`), Dark Elevation Slate (`#131315`), Electric Lime Accent (`#e2ff00`), Emerald Status (`#10b981`).
- **Motion**: Minimal, restrained spring physics curves (`250ms` duration cap).

## Avoid (Strict Anti-Patterns)
- ❌ Avoid cheap glassmorphic overlays
- ❌ Avoid generic purple or synthetic SaaS gradients
- ❌ Avoid overused SaaS grid cards & decorative icon boxes
- ❌ Avoid synthetic marketing hype badges

# UI/UX Pro Max Skill

> **Core Purpose**: Provide production-ready, industry-specific design stacks, font pairings, color palettes, and component blueprints for any web application or landing page.

---

## 1. INDUSTRY DESIGN STACKS & PALETTES

### A. Specialty Coffee / Ultra Luxury Monolith
- **Display Font**: Playfair Display (Serif)
- **Body Font**: Inter (Sans-Serif)
- **Palette**: Obsidian `#0A0A0B` (Background), Vermilion `#FF4500` (Primary Accent), Warm Sand `#D4A373` (Supporting), Off-White `#FDFBF7` (Text).
- **Vibe**: Dark, editorial, high-contrast, kinetic scroll motion.

### B. B2B Technical SaaS / AI Developer Tool
- **Display Font**: JetBrains Mono or SF Pro Display
- **Body Font**: Inter or Geist
- **Palette**: Charcoal `#0D0E12` (Background), Electric Indigo `#5E6AD2` (Primary), Mint `#10B981` (Accent), Cool Gray `#F7F8F8` (Text).
- **Vibe**: Linear-style, minimalist, crisp borders, restrained spring motion.

### C. Luxury Real Estate / High-End Property
- **Display Font**: Bodoni Moda or Cormorant Garamond
- **Body Font**: Neue Haas Grotesk or Outfit
- **Palette**: Emerald Obsidian `#071C17` (Background), Gold `#D4AF37` (Primary), Sand `#F4EFEA` (Surface), Pure Cream `#FCFAEE` (Text).
- **Vibe**: Architectural, expansive hero images, subtle parallax, glass overlays.

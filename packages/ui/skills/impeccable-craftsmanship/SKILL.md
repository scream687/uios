# Impeccable Craftsmanship Skill

> **Core Purpose**: Enforce exact, pixel-perfect typography and layout craftsmanship rules distilled from top design agencies (Apple, Linear, Stripe).

---

## 1. OPTICAL TYPOGRAPHY RULES

1. **Headline Optical Tracking**:
   - Headlines $\ge 48\text{px}$: Set tracking to **-0.03em** to **-0.05em**.
   - Micro-caps / Category Pills $\le 12\text{px}$: Set tracking to **+0.12em** to **+0.18em** (uppercase).
2. **Line Height Ratios**:
   - Display Headlines: **1.05** to **1.10** line height.
   - Body Paragraphs: **1.50** to **1.65** line height.
3. **Reading Measure**:
   - Constrain body paragraph width between **45ch** and **75ch** (optimal reading velocity).

---

## 2. WHITESPACE & SECTION RHYTHM

1. **Height Variance**:
   - Section heights must vary by $\ge 300\text{px}$ across sequential sections to prevent monotone scroll fatigue.
2. **Hero Viewport Scale**:
   - Hero section must occupy $\ge 80\text{vh}$ on desktop viewports.
3. **Asymmetry Ratio**:
   - Maintain asymmetry ratio $\ge 0.28$ between primary hero focal objects and supporting narrative copy.

---

## 3. COLOR & CONTRAST THRESHOLDS

1. **No Pure Blacks or Pure Whites**:
   - Replace `#000000` with obsidian `#0A0A0B`.
   - Replace `#FFFFFF` with warm off-white cream `#FDFBF7`.
2. **Contrast Ratios**:
   - Body text: $\ge 7.0:1$ WCAG AAA contrast ratio.
   - Headlines: $\ge 4.5:1$ contrast ratio.

# Coffee Industry Design Intelligence

## Core Design Principles for Coffee Roasting & Specialty Coffee

1. **Dominant Physical Object Anchor**:
   - Hero section MUST feature a single, high-contrast physical object (Volcanic Coffee Bag, Anaerobic Fermentation Vat, or Single-Origin Extraction Viewport) occupying >= 80% viewport surface area.

2. **Editorial Negative Space**:
   - Maintain generous vertical whitespace (Y padding >= 144px).
   - Use high-contrast editorial typography pairing (e.g. Playfair Display + Inter).

3. **Immersive Terroir Storytelling**:
   - Transition seamlessly from high-altitude elevation maps (1,500m - 2,400m) to anaerobic vat fermentation chrono telemetry.

4. **Restrained Chromatic Accent**:
   - Background: Obsidian Dark (#0A0A0B) or Off-White Cream (#FDFBF7).
   - Accent: Volcanic Vermilion (#FF4500) or Roasted Ochre (#D4A373).

# Luxury Brand Psychology Design Intelligence

## Core Design Principles for Premium & Luxury Brands

1. **Extreme Asymmetry & Spatial Bleed**:
   - Avoid symmetric 3-column grids. Use asymmetrical 8:4 or 7:5 column spans with rotational tension (1.0° - 1.5°).

2. **Subtle Micro-Motion**:
   - Micro-animations duration: 150ms - 250ms with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing.
   - Zero motion clutter or gaudy bouncing.

3. **High Optical Hierarchy**:
   - Contrast ratio exceeds 6.5:1.
   - Negative tracking (-0.03em) on headlines; uppercase tracking (+0.15em) on small section metadata pills.
